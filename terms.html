Terms and money-back guarantee

Prelaunch policies · Paid signup is closed. Undebtr currently runs locally; this website does not host the application or financial records.

Undebtr is operated by THE EAGLE GROUP ADVANTAGE LLC. Contact: joseph.aguilar@tegadv.com.

Current availability. Undebtr is in prelaunch preparation. The application currently runs locally, public paid signup is closed, and this website does not host the financial database. Do not send payment details or bank credentials by email. These pages describe the intended paid service; they are not an invitation to purchase before signup opens.

What the software does. Undebtr organizes financial records and models budgets, bills, goals, cash flow and debt-payoff scenarios. Calculations depend on the records and assumptions supplied. Verify results against original statements before acting. Undebtr does not lend money, settle debts, manage investments, transfer money or promise financial outcomes. The software does not replace individualized financial, tax, investment or legal advice.

Prices and renewal. When paid signup opens, Connected will be offered at USD $9.99 per month or USD $79.99 per year. The selected period is charged at the start of that period and renews until canceled. Checkout must display the selected cadence, total and any applicable tax before purchase. Bank connection allowances, provider availability and the actual service delivery arrangement must be stated before a customer buys. No unlimited bank connections are promised. Assisted setup is not currently available to purchase.

Cancellation. Use the Stripe customer portal to cancel future renewal or contact joseph.aguilar@tegadv.com. No separate cancellation fee applies. Ordinary cancellation preserves paid access until the displayed period end. Cancellation does not delete financial records. For paid Connected service, bank connections are scheduled for disconnection 30 days after the subscription ends. Maintenance runs while the service is running; offline or unsuccessful removals are retried when service resumes. Active renewed subscriptions prevent expiry disconnection. Local manual records and exports remain available after Connected ends.

Money-back guarantee. At any point after purchase, a customer may request a prorated refund for unused time remaining in the current paid subscription period, including after a renewal. Email joseph.aguilar@tegadv.com or submit an in-app Help request with the billing reference and a brief reason. A reason is required; no proof of a software defect is required. The unused portion is calculated from the time a complete refund request is received, capped at the amount paid for that period after earlier refunds. When the refund is issued, the subscription is canceled, future renewal stops, and paid Connected access ends immediately. Local records and exports remain available. Do not delete the account before requesting a refund. Fraud or abuse of the service or promotions may make a request ineligible. Mandatory consumer rights are unaffected. No separate cancellation or refund fee is charged. If app-store purchases are offered in the future, their refunds must follow the store's process; Undebtr currently has no app-store billing.

Providers and connections. Bank connections are optional and require separate consent through Plaid and the bank. Availability, historical coverage, refresh timing and accuracy can vary. Stripe handles billing and card information. Undebtr stores provider identifiers and subscription status rather than card numbers. Disconnecting a bank revokes the application's access token; previously imported records can remain until removed by the account owner.

Support and changes. Support is available through the business email and the local application's Help workflow. No staffed hours, 24/7 coverage, guaranteed response time or uptime guarantee is currently offered. Any future price or material service changes will be communicated before the affected renewal; they will not silently change an already-paid period. Customers may stop renewal before a change takes effect.
