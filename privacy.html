Privacy and data handling

Prelaunch policies · Paid signup is closed. Undebtr currently runs locally; this website does not host the application or financial records.

Undebtr is operated by THE EAGLE GROUP ADVANTAGE LLC. Send privacy, access or deletion questions to joseph.aguilar@tegadv.com. Do not include full account numbers, passwords or government identifiers in ordinary email or support requests.

Local records. The current application stores account, transaction, budget, goal, planning and support records on the installation computer. The local operator controls that machine and its encryption keys. Local storage is not protection against someone who has authorized access to the computer and its keys. This public documentation website does not host the application or its financial database.

Optional services. Google processes sign-in requests and provides the selected basic profile and verified email. Plaid handles bank authentication and shares the accounts and activity the customer authorizes. Stripe processes billing identity, payment details, subscriptions and invoices. Their privacy policies and legal obligations govern the records they maintain. Undebtr does not store customer card numbers or collect bank passwords in its own forms.

Sharing and support. Undebtr does not sell personal financial data or use it for advertising. Household and reviewer access is limited to the access the account owner explicitly grants. Support operators can read submitted support requests and replies through the support interface; that role does not grant blanket financial-record access. System administrators may have infrastructure access to a system they operate.

Exports, removal and backups. Financial exports are available on the local installation. Account owners can disconnect bank access and remove records through the application where supported. Privacy requests concerning operator-held records can be sent to the business email. Automatic local financial backup rotation retains up to seven copies; separately downloaded exports and owner-created backups remain under their holder's control. Removing a current record does not automatically erase earlier independent backups or provider-held billing records. Required provider retention and applicable legal obligations can prevent immediate deletion of some provider records. No off-device backup location or retention schedule is promised for a future hosted service; any such service requires updated disclosures before launch.

Documentation website. The public policy pages collect no form submissions and contain no application-controlled advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. The website host can process request logs and visitor IP addresses under its own privacy policy. Contacting support or a provider separately creates records with that recipient.

Policy version: November 16, 2026. Operator: THE EAGLE GROUP ADVANTAGE LLC.
