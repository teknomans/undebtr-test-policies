Undebtr

PRELAUNCH PRODUCT PREVIEW

A clearer plan for your money. A path through debt.

Undebtr is personal finance software under development. The local application currently uses the Vantage name. It brings financial records, budgeting, cash-flow forecasts and debt-payoff scenarios into one workspace.

What the local application offers

  • Manual accounts, transactions and statement imports.
  • Budgets, goals, bills and cash-flow planning.
  • Debt-payoff comparisons and saved financial scenarios.
  • Financial exports and encrypted backups.
  • Optional bank connections through Plaid, with separate user sign-in and consent.

The software organizes and models records. It does not move money, offer credit or guarantee financial outcomes. Provider availability and bank-data quality can vary.

Availability and billing

The application currently runs locally on the operator's computer. Public signup and real subscription payments are closed. Stripe integration is being tested with synthetic data in an isolated sandbox. This page does not host the finance app or its database.

Planned Connected pricing is USD $7.99 per month or $59.99 per year. These are proposed prelaunch prices, not a current offer to purchase. Commercial terms, business contact information and the final refund policy will be published before real subscriptions open.
