Sandbox test terms

Use only synthetic customer information and Stripe-provided test payment details in the designated Undebtr sandbox. Do not enter a real card, bank credential, government identifier, or other sensitive personal information into a synthetic test.

The tested software organizes financial records and models budgets, bills, cash flow, goals and debt scenarios. It does not move money, provide individualized financial advice, or guarantee balances, savings or repayment outcomes.

Sample Connected subscription prices are USD $7.99 per month and $59.99 per year. These are sandbox fixtures. No real purchase, charge, renewal, refund obligation or service entitlement is created by this test. Test trials may be cancelled and synthetic test records retained as verification evidence.

The test customer portal is intended to demonstrate invoice access, payment-method updates and cancellation at the end of a sample billing period. Checkout success alone does not grant access; the application verifies subscription state with Stripe.

Commercial signup remains closed. Before any real offer, the operator must publish approved commercial terms identifying the legal service provider, support contact, renewal and cancellation conditions, refund policy and applicable consumer rights. These test terms must not be substituted for that agreement.

Sandbox test privacy notice

This static page collects no form submissions and includes no analytics scripts, advertising pixels or cookies controlled by the application. GitHub Pages, the page host, may process visitor IP addresses and request logs for security under GitHub's privacy statement.

The finance application and personal database run on the operator's computer. Neither is hosted on this website. This public repository contains only test-policy documentation, not personal financial records, application credentials, database copies or bank tokens.

Stripe processes synthetic test customer, checkout and subscription information in its sandbox under Stripe's privacy policy. Isolated local test databases and provider-side synthetic records may be retained as testing evidence. No automatic deletion period is promised.

For the local application, bank connections remain optional and require separate user sign-in and consent. No bank connection is required to view this page or run synthetic Stripe tests.

Documentation for testing only. No public paid signup is available.
